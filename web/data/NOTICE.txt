Offline context map

Made with Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/).
Natural Earth raster and vector datasets are in the public domain.
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

These files derive from the natural-earth-vector v5.1.2 release. Attributes
were reduced and state, lake, and place records were selected as documented
in manifest.json and ../../docs/map-sources.md. Retained coordinates were
not simplified, rounded, or otherwise changed by this project.

Natural Earth is a generalized context map. It does not establish PLSS
coverage, ownership, surveyed boundaries, or the accuracy of lookup results.
The separately loaded BLM atlas supplies the PLSS geometry used for lookup.
